About
I take “in the wild” robotics literally.

My name is Greta, and I am currently applying for a PhD in Mechanical Engineering for Fall 2027. I graduated with my MSc in Robotics from NYU in 2026, and my BSc in Mechanical Engineering & AI from RPI in 2024. I'm researching embodied intelligence for field robotics: from applying foundation models to marine archaeology and conservation as a researcher at the NYU Center for Robotics & Embodied Intelligence, to teaching robots how to manipulate clothing intelligently at Silana. Follow me on my journey to make field robotics intelligent.
