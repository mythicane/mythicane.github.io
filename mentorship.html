Mentorship

Students and mentees I've worked with, and how.

I founded RSEI (Rensselaer Society of Engineering Innovation) circa 2021, blossoming from a demand for inclusive organizations that cater towards radical inventors, audacious engineers, and tenacious entrepreneurs. The organization, running out of the National Historic Landmark Rensselaer Society of Engineers Clubhouse (Wikipedia), was the fastest growing organization during my tenure, growing from 1 to over 50 brilliant members. After graduating from RPI, I now volunteer as an Alumni Director at RSEI.
